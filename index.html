🤖 What is Smartbotics Bot

It's like hiring a sales rep
who never sleeps, never misses a message,
and speaks every language.

Smartbotics Bot is an AI-powered WhatsApp assistant that connects to your business WhatsApp number. It reads every incoming message, understands what the customer wants, and replies — in seconds — with the right answer, product info, or next step.

When a conversation needs a real human (a complex order, an urgent issue, an enterprise deal), the bot hands over to your team — with the full chat history — so nothing gets lost.

You set it up once. It works every day, every hour, forever.
